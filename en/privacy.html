Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-14

Pipewick is designed to work without collecting personal data.

1. No accounts, no personal data

Pipewick requires no account, no sign-in and no registration. The game itself does not collect, store or transmit your name, e-mail address, contacts, location or any other personal information.

2. Everything stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, hint balance, daily streak, settings and purchase state are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device through the game, and deleting the app deletes it. There is no cloud backup operated by us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pipewick shows a rewarded video only when you explicitly choose to watch one (for example to earn a free hint). There are no banner ads and no ads during gameplay. Rewarded videos are served by Google AdMob, a third-party service. When an ad is shown, Google may process device information (such as the advertising identifier) to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy. If you buy Pipewick PRO, no ads are shown at all and therefore no ad-related processing occurs.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases (hint packs and Pipewick PRO) are processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; the stores only confirm to the app which products you own.

5. Children

Because the game collects no personal data, no personal data of children is collected either. Rewarded ads are only shown after an explicit tap on an ad button.

6. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated text ships inside an app update; the date above always shows the latest revision.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to mnpekdemir.apps@gmail.com.
